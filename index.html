Memvella

Memory support companion

Source

Memvella is a voice-first memory support companion for people in early-stage cognitive decline and the family members supporting them. It combines a gentle tablet experience for routines, memories, and prompts with carer-side tools for adding context, coordinating support, and keeping the person's life story close; it won the £500 Ignite award from the Surrey Innovation and Enterprise Hub.
